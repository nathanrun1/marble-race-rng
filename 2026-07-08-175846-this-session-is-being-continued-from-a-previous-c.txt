● Playtest done (full fresh-join run: tutorial → upgrades → free box → loadout →
  AUTO grind). Here's the diagnosis.

  TL;DR

  The median 2-minute session is almost exactly the tutorial length plus one
  bust. The tutorial itself walks players into the bust: it ends by raising
  their bet to $40 with a build (Mult +5%, loadout +4%) that leaves board EV at
  ~0.95–1.0×. Every track that actually flips the board net-positive is behind
  the $1,000 Red Lock. So a new player's first autonomous minutes are a
  high-variance coin flip they can't influence — I watched my balance go $505 →
  $174 in 50 seconds on AUTO, then swing back up on a heater. The unlucky half
  busts and quits; the lucky half becomes your prestige-100 whales. The game
  isn't too easy — it's too punishing and too opaque in minutes 2–5, and the
  loop only gets interesting after the locks.

  What I saw, minute by minute

  1. Launching feels blind. From the launch platform the board is nearly
  edge-on; the "Watch where the marble drops…" tutorial step plays without
  showing you the board. The proper board camera exists but is behind an
  unlabeled eye icon the tutorial never mentions.
  2. The tutorial's economic endpoint is a trap. It teaches Mult Bonus ($300,
  +5%) and Max Bet ($250 → bet jumps to $40), then sends you to the free box.
  Nothing teaches that the board bleeds without the right build, and the right
  build isn't buyable: FlatRate/PegHeat/Frequency are behind Red Lock ($1K),
  Angle/Arc/Elasticity/Size behind Blue Lock ($5K). 7 of 9 kiosks are grey ❓ 
  and the locks show a price but not what's inside — so early "build expression"
  is literally two buttons: raise bet, raise mult.
  3. Recovery is waiting, not playing. After a bust your income is coins (every
  30–60s), playtime bonus (3/8 min, hidden in a panel), and the unsignposted
  obby. The game's answer to "I'm broke" is "stand around."
  4. Wins don't land visually. Score/combo text renders as tiny lines in the
  bottom-right corner, spatially disconnected from the slot the marble hit. The
  tier-0 board is also drab grey-on-grey — the weakest presentation at the most
  critical moment (it presumably gets prettier with prestige, i.e., for people
  already retained).
  5. What already works: the free-box pad → "NEW! Rose" → equip-best beat is
  genuinely good dopamine; luck rank ticks Noob→Rookie→Amateur fast; quests pay
  diamonds. These are the bones of a good first session — they're just not
  sequenced into one.

  Recommendations, in order of expected impact

  A. Stop the post-tutorial bust (biggest lever for median session):
  1. Don't leave new players at bet $40. Buying the Max Bet ceiling shouldn't
  auto-raise the active bet — or the tutorial should end by teaching Raise/Lower
  and setting them back to $10.
  2. A one-time, visible "Beginner's Luck" buff — e.g. +15% payout for the first
  15 minutes of a new account. It puts early EV just above 1 without reopening
  the bet-spam exploit (it's per-account and time-limited), and its countdown is
  itself a "stay past 5 minutes" hook.
  3. Make the Red Lock reachable in the first ~3 minutes: drop it to ~$500, or
  start Frequency/FlatRate unlocked and reserve locks for the exotic tracks.
  Your own sim's greedy buy order says these are the levers that make play
  skillful; right now skill is locked.

  B. Make the loop legible:
  4. Tutorial should swap to the board camera on the "watch it drop" step (or
  highlight the eye icon). Players who never find that view never see the game.
  5. Locks should advertise contents ("🔒 Unlocks: Launch Speed, Combo Pay, Peg
  Heat") — turns a grey wall of ❓ into a goal.
  6. Move score FX onto the board: a world-space popup at the slot the marble
  landed in, sized by payout. The corner text wastes your best juice.

  C. Give the session a spine (targets the 21%-past-5-min number directly):
  7. A one-time "Getting Started" quest chain (reuse QuestService): Launch 10
  marbles → Buy 3 upgrades → Collect 5 coins → Open the Red Lock → Prestige.
  Each step claimable, each step ~1–2 minutes apart. This is the cheapest big
  win — the infrastructure exists.
  8. Surface the timers you already have as HUD chips: free box ("🎁 2:55") and
  next playtime bonus. Both are currently invisible unless you open panels;
  visible countdowns are the classic "just 2 more minutes" retainer.

  D. Depth for the mid-game (the one-dimensionality worry):
  9. Confirmed from the June analysis: aim/size barely move the slot
  distribution, so builds differ in numbers, not behavior. The roadmap items —
  rotating board events on the day/night cycle, prestige-unlocked second board —
  are the real fix; golden pegs already help. Raising VJackFraction so the
  jackpot gate requires a heat build would make "targeting" a genuine archetype
  with zero new content.
  10. Give the tier-0 board a friendlier palette. First impressions shouldn't be
  the drabbest tier.

  E. Two UI bugs found while playing: the kiosk ShopUI panel never auto-closes
  when you walk away (it sat in screen center across the whole map until I hit
  X), and the Playtime Bonus panel opens on top of the loadout trigger that the
  loadout tutorial highlights — a player with that panel open can get stuck
  mid-tutorial.

  If you want a single first move: A1 + C7 — stop handing new players a $40
  bleed, and give them a quest ladder that reaches past minute 5. Happy to
  implement any of these; the quest chain and the bet fix are both small.
